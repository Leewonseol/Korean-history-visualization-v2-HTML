https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0028169

Seojeongnok should be treated separately from the Sillok:
- Current surviving woodblock edition was published in 1516.
- It concerns Yi Cheon (李蕆), the Pajŏ River campaigns, Four Counties,
  and events from 1432 through 1437.
- According to the encyclopedia description, it includes military campaigns,
  migration/resettlement, taxation, relief, local reports, and court responses.
- Do NOT treat the encyclopedia summary itself as the primary text.

F. REQUIRED DATA PRINCIPLES
==================================================

1. EVENT DATE != RECORD DATE
If a report dated May describes an April battle, preserve both.

2. CLAIM != FACT
Yi Manju, Menggetemur, Ming, Joseon and other actors often report incompatible accounts.

3. MILITARY OUTCOME NUMBERS
Treat battlefield kill/capture counts as reported figures attributed to the reporting side.

4. RESETTLEMENT
Encode benefits AND coercion:
- relief
- farmland
- defense
- forced relocation
- punishment for evasion
- mortality

5. FEEDBACK LOOPS
Build temporal paths such as:

field intelligence
-> Kim Jong-seo
-> royal court
-> policy decision
-> frontier commander
-> local military action
-> outcome
-> report
-> court
-> reward / punishment / revised policy

6. DO NOT MERGE:
- all Jurchens
- all Udige / Oryanghap / Odori
- Yi Manju's group with Menggetemur/Fancha automatically
- Pajŏ River campaign with the entire Four Counties/Six Garrisons process

7. NO PROTAGONIST RANKING YET
Compute trajectories and role transitions.
Human author decides protagonist.
